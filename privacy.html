Privacy Policy – Orbit Breaker (Android)

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (applies from version 1.1.0)

Controller: Max Heissenberger, Bruckneudorf, Austria, e-mail: maxheissenberger@gmail.com

Summary

Orbit Breaker is free and is funded by a few, fair ads:

  • Rewarded videos play only when you tap them yourself (e.g. for an extra life, double coins or the daily stardust chest).
  • Full-screen ads are rare: from level 15 at the earliest, at most after every 4th cleared level and at least 5 minutes apart. Never after a loss and never right before or after a boss.
  • There are no banners.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The one-time in-app purchase "Remove ads" (via Google Play) turns full-screen ads off.

We run no servers of our own, there is no user account, and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own. Your progress stays on your device. The game also works offline; you then simply get no ads and no ad-funded rewards.

1. Progress and settings (local)

Level progress, medals, coins, stars, unlocked content, language, and sound, vibration and display options are stored only locally in the app's private storage. This includes simple counters for the ad rules, such as when the last full-screen ad played, and whether "Remove ads" was purchased.

None of this is sent to us.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

To serve, count and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app processes, among other things:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived (no GPS);
  • device and app information, such as device model, OS, language and app version;
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, watched rewarded videos);
  • diagnostics and performance data, such as load times and SDK crashes.

This data is sent to Google encrypted (HTTPS) and is used by Google for advertising: serving and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, if you consent, personalisation.

Legal bases:

  • your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the ePrivacy rules for device access) for personalised ads;
  • our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads where permitted.

More information:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Ad content is limited to the "PG" maturity rating.

3. Consent (Google UMP / "Privacy settings")

On first launch, where required for your region (e.g. EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app asks for your consent to personalised ads using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your consent choice is stored locally on the device and passed to AdMob.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings. If you decline, you will still see ads, but non-personalised or limited ones.

4. Advertising ID

The app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission so the ads SDK can read the advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads). After that, no personalised ads are served based on the advertising ID.

5. In-app purchase: Google Play Billing

The "Remove ads" purchase (one-time, not a subscription) is handled by Google Play. You enter payment details only with Google. We receive no payment data. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can be restored after a reinstall.

Google Play's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, consent and the purchase.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): see section 4.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): for the Google Play purchase.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, can be turned off in the settings.
  • Other technical permissions the ads SDK adds automatically, for example to keep the device awake briefly or to react to network changes. These give no access to personal content.

There is no access to contacts, precise location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, e.g. "Google One backup", Android may back up the game progress with your other app data to your own Google account. Google controls this backup. We have no access to it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Data held by Google: Google's retention and deletion rules apply to advertising and purchase data. You can manage it in your Google account (https://myaccount.google.com), e.g. under "Data & privacy" and "My Ad Center".
  • With us: We store no data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may complain to a supervisory authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (https://www.dsb.gv.at).

We hold no data on servers of our own, so please contact Google directly about data held by Google. For anything else, feel free to write to us.

10. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to the "PG" rating.

11. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above.
